01 / SYSTEM VIEW

How I approach products

The product is bigger than the schematic.

A product succeeds only when the electrical design, software behavior, interfaces, test strategy, supply chain, manufacturing process, and customer use case work together.

That is why my roles have repeatedly expanded across disciplines: from software into embedded systems, from individual products into product portfolios, and from engineering teams into global delivery and factory readiness.

03 / NPI + FACTORY

Where product development becomes real

A design is not ready because the CAD is done.

Build readiness includes materials, process, fixtures, test, work instructions, acceptance criteria, ownership, and a plan for what the team will learn from the build.

BEFORE

Prepare

Design maturity, CTB, materials, manufacturing plan, test readiness, process readiness.

DURING

Observe

Yield, defects, cycle time, test results, blockers, containment, ownership.

AFTER

Close

Root cause, corrective action, evidence, learning, next-build entry criteria.

I see NPI as part of engineering—not a handoff after engineering. The build is one of the strongest sources of evidence about whether the product and the development system are actually ready.
