01 / THE IDEA

Why I started it

AI should work with engineering tools, not just generate answers.

In my day-to-day work, I began exploring AI-assisted tools for requirements, technical research, design artifacts, calculations, and validation. These tools complement existing engineering and PLM systems rather than replace them.

Universe extends that work into a broader development framework, exploring what an AI-assisted development environment could look like when agents can work with engineering state and engineering tools—not just a conversation.

02 / ARCHITECTURE

Connect reasoning to engineering state

How the pieces fit together.

Universe separates AI reasoning, engineering state, execution, domain logic, external software, and engineer review.

Canonical Engineering Models (CEMs)

A CEM is a structured, versioned representation of engineering entities, relationships, properties, and evidence. It gives agents, domain tools, and engineers a shared design state while keeping revisions and evidence explicit.

03 / WHAT I HAVE BUILT

From concept to working pieces

Several parts are already working.

REQUIREMENTS → ARCHITECTURE

Turn reviewed requirements into a system concept

An implemented workflow turns source-linked requirements into a proposed system structure, subsystem responsibilities, interfaces, constraints, and owned unknowns. Revisions stay attributable, and the candidate remains separate from engineering approval.

ENGINEERING TOOLS

Calculations, geometry + engineering software

Implemented work includes electrical and power calculations, component screening, system interfaces, geometry, bounded thermal and scenario studies, validation, and FreeCAD / OCCT integration.

TRACEABILITY

Results stay tied to their basis

Inputs, sources, revisions, and execution evidence stay attached to the work. When the basis changes, affected results can be identified for re-evaluation instead of silently remaining current.

WORKBENCH

Review the engineering work, not just the conversation

A React / TypeScript review environment brings together requirements, models, calculations, evidence, proposed changes, open issues, and 3D visualization.

Implementation stack

Rust supports Core and Runtime. Python connects agents, domain tools, and engineering software. Workbench uses TypeScript / React and Three.js.

04 / CONCRETE EXAMPLE

Component selection

A shortlist is a proposal—not a design decision.

One implemented workflow connects project requirements, candidate discovery, source evidence, domain screening, and engineer review.

The workflow records the requirement basis, project revision, comparison result, and evidence used. If that basis changes, the prior recommendation remains in history but requires re-evaluation.

Current status

Universe is an active project, not a finished or production-qualified platform. It gives me a practical way to explore how AI agents, engineering tools, structured models, evidence, and human review can work together in product development.
