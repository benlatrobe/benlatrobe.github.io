01 / THE IDEA

Why I started it

AI should work with engineering tools, not just generate answers.

In my day-to-day work, I began exploring AI-assisted tools for requirements, technical research, design artifacts, calculations, and validation. These tools complement existing engineering and PLM systems rather than replace them.

Universe extends that work into a broader development framework, exploring what an AI-assisted development environment could look like when agents can work with engineering state and engineering tools—not just a conversation.

02 / ARCHITECTURE

Connect reasoning to engineering state

How the pieces fit together.

Universe separates AI reasoning, engineering state, execution, domain logic, external software, and engineer review.

Canonical Engineering Models (CEMs)

A CEM is a structured, versioned engineering record that connects entities, relationships, properties, and source evidence. For supported component and assembly models, it retains the exact geometry needed for reconstruction. CAD exports and display meshes are derived outputs—not competing design authorities.

03 / WHAT I HAVE BUILT

From architecture to working engineering workflows

Connecting AI proposals to engineering work.

REQUIREMENTS → CONCEPT

From project documents to a reviewable concept

A verified local Workbench journey connects source-linked requirements, clarification, and reviewed scope to model-authored planning and saved concept results. Assumptions and unfinished obligations remain visible.

ENGINEERING MODELS → CAD

Native CAD, checked changes, and component reuse

Bounded workflows turn saved concepts into reviewable native CAD and check explicit position changes. Separately, exact library Component CEM revisions can enter a project for reviewed placement, comparison, and STEP / FCStd export.

TRACEABILITY

Versioned state and evidence that survives a restart

Results stay tied to source inputs, engineering revisions, tool checks, and saved review context. Existing and changed candidates remain distinguishable; guarded reads can restore their evidence without rerunning engineering tools.

ENGINEERING WORKBENCH

Inspect the model, not just an AI answer

A React / TypeScript / Three.js interface supports project and requirements review, 3D selection, saved views, engineering checks, reviewed changes, and contextual discussion tied to specific records.

Implementation stack

Rust supports Core and Runtime. Python connects agents, domain tools, and native engineering software. Workbench uses TypeScript / React and Three.js.

04 / CONCRETE EXAMPLE

Concept to checked CAD change

AI proposes. Engineering tools check. Engineers decide.

One locally verified workflow takes a saved model-authored placement, applies declared 3D assumptions, and creates native CAD that an engineer can inspect and review.

Engineer review is required

AI proposals and CAD checks support decisions; they do not approve the design.

A reviewed component translation produces a separate candidate with new spatial checks. The original geometry, saved views, and contextual evidence remain available for comparison. This is assisted concept review—not autonomous CAD design or approval of an installed product.

Current status

Universe remains in active development. These workflows have bounded local verification using synthetic engineering cases. Geometry screens, successful executions, and native exports do not by themselves qualify installed fit, safety, or engineering release.
