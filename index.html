03 / LEADERSHIP

What experience changed my view

The hardest engineering problems are often not purely technical.

They are problems of ownership, priorities, interfaces, communication, and decision-making.

I have led engineering organizations across the U.S. and Asia, spanning electrical engineering, software and firmware, mechanical engineering, validation, project management, and cross-functional delivery.

01

Make ownership visible.

Ambiguity becomes delay. Clear decision rights and interfaces are part of the design.

02

Connect the system.

Product, engineering, suppliers, manufacturing, and customers need a shared operating picture.

03

Use evidence.

Good execution gets easier when status, risk, validation, and readiness can be inspected—not interpreted.
