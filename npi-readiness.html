RELEASE GATE02

Do not release a process that is not capable

MISS FPY / Cpk→HOLD RELEASE→TUNE PROCESS→REVALIDATE

Held production release when a dispensing process missed its capability gate, then drove supplier tuning and revalidation until the process met acceptance criteria.

FACTORY TRANSFER03

Localization is also process redesign

TRANSFER→SIMPLIFY→POINT-OF-USE→LESS VARIATION

Supported manufacturing localization by replacing labor-intensive packaging steps with pre-formed point-of-use assemblies to reduce handling, cycle time, and process variation.
